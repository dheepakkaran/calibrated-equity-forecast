Why these read lower than a terminal's. The correlation is against the driver's lagged series — the value that was knowable at the Indian close. A same-day silver-versus-stock correlation is far higher and completely unusable for forecasting the session it belongs to.
Reading this screen. Attribution is correlational, not causal. A high score means a filing was found close in time whose materiality and direction fit the move — not that it caused it. Low-confidence and unexplained rows are shown rather than hidden, because a move nobody can explain is itself information.

Design note. Colour is rationed on purpose. Teal and rose stay desaturated at low confidence and only gain saturation above 60%, which is rare — the model's confidence has never exceeded about 55%. Amber is reserved for uncertainty. The result is a dashboard that looks calm when the model has nothing useful to say, which is most days: on the latest session it declines to call 39 of 52 symbols.
