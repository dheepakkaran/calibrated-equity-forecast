Calibrated Selective Prediction / NSE

Research system · educational use only

This tells you what a model thinks, and how little it knows.

It forecasts one thing: whether an NSE share will out-perform or under-perform the median large-cap in the next session. On held-out data it is right 51.0% of the time against a 50.0% coin flip.

That is a real edge and a small one. Most days it has nothing useful to say, and on those days it says so.

Not investment advice. No trades are executed.
